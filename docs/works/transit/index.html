02 / 2023 / Site-specific virtual reality installation

Transit

Inside, outside, elsewhere

Manuel Eguía / Mauro Zannoli

A virtual journey begins in an exact replica of the room the participant occupies. Familiar space gradually opens into impossible interiors and transformed landscapes.

Transit is conceived as a series of site-specific virtual reality experiences. Each episode starts from the participant’s immediate physical surroundings, making the transition into a virtual world difficult to locate. Local architecture, landscape and cultural references become the material of the work.

The first episode inhabits iM Konsthall, a mobile gallery built inside a shipping container. Its interior multiplies and transforms through a sequence of spatial folds, until the participant finds themself outside, in the northern Swedish forest at night, looking for the way back.

Medium
Site-specific virtual reality installation
My role
Co-creator with Mauro Zannoli.
Context
Commissioned by Northern Sustainable Futures for iM Konsthall. Developed during an artist residency in Moskosel, Sweden, October 2023.

Returning virtual space to a place

The container is both the physical venue and an image of transit. Its windows initially frame the same landscape seen before putting on the headset. That continuity provides the perceptual anchor from which the installation departs.

Transit brings territory and context into digital experience. The journey returns to its starting point, but the passage through altered spaces changes the participant’s relationship to that familiar room.
